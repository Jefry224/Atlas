## Findings and recommended repairs

1. **High: AI context silently loses the information needed for decisions.** `server/copilot.py:64 flatten_context` stops at 400 leaves and truncates each list at 24 items, using insertion order without reserving room for later sections. Read-only probe of a real lava-tube scenario, archived telemetry and forecast, `automaticEngineering`, and `buildDossierContext` produced exactly 400 leaves, ending at `analogyAudit.calibration.inputs.earthGravityMs2`: **zero processed-process, hazards, or unavailableModules leaves survived**. This is not just a long hypothetical payload: forecast hours consume 198 leaves ahead of the audit; at most 24 of the offered 72 hourly entries survive. A second probe reconstructed `mission.js:1890 buildCopilotContext` using saved `nominal-jezero-mission.json` evidence and `compactLanding`: it ended at `analogyAudit.roles.target`, dropping **the entire audit matrix, automatic calibration, final limitation and processes**. Local rules consume the full object, so successful local copilot evidence does not verify the AI path. Repair with an explicit compact provider DTO and section budgets: prioritize mission/site identity, current objective/route/payload/evidence/result, audit conclusions, selected processed windows, provenance and missing-data notices; omit raw paths and redundant profile metadata; include truncation/coverage metadata. Do not simply increase every limit or place the new Earth expedition last. Add a targeted real-shape serialization assertion proving these sections reach both provider prompts.

2. **High: stale asynchronous M5 dossier results can be attributed to a different scenario.** `dossier.js:108 loadScenario` sets global `state.scenario`, starts asynchronous grids and weather/forecast, then reads that mutable global again to construct the candidate/context. A late A response after B is selected can build B scenario identifiers with A grids; adaptationWarnings may invalidate the audit but do not prevent context publication or generation. There is no generation token/abort guard, no clearing of context while loading, and Generate can use the previous context. `generate:145` publishes an old request after switching; `downloadMarkdown:159` names it with the current scenario ID. The error path also stores a truthy context despite saying the dossier cannot be generated safely, and `generate` checks only truthiness. Repair: capture the selected scenario locally, clear context/dossier immediately, increment a selection generation, abort superseded requests, gate assignment after every await, disable Generate until the matching context is ready, and bind generation/output/download metadata to an immutable scenario/context identity. Add reverse-completion A/B and switch-during-generation checks. Coordinator independently flagged this same race and is browser-reproducing it with controlled delays; this review independently confirms the source paths, but has not run that browser reproduction.

3. **High for Earth mode: copilot and conversation history ignore the active mission/site.** `mission.js:1890 buildCopilotContext` always reads `S.scenario`/`S.sc`, even in catalogue Earth Terrain; `selectSite:306`, `activateAtlasTerrain:949` and `setView:885` do not replace that destination context. Selecting another Earth site after a destination mission therefore sends the old analogue/target, audit and process analysis together with the current rover battery. Remote-added `S.copilotHistory` (71,1910,1946) is never reset or scoped by scenario/session/site, and a late answer is appended to shared history after switches. Repair with one active mission context dispatcher keyed by mode + session + site/grid revision; use Earth expedition/site data in Terrain, destination data in split, and explicit unavailable state on globe/loading. Scope history and pending answers to that identity. Preserve the remote provider/history feature instead of removing it. Verify with a recorded POST body: Jezero -> a different Earth site -> ask about route/battery/site; neither old mission evidence nor its history may appear.

4. **Medium: exported mission identifies two different Earth sites.** `mission.js:1106 export-mission` always builds `site` from catalogue `S.siteId`, even in split, while `scenario` and `activeGrid()` come from the selected destination mission. Existing `docs/evidencias/preparacion-rendimiento/nominal-jezero-mission.json` concretely exports `site.id=pampa-joya`, Peru coordinates, but `scenario.earthId=atacama` and an Atacama candidate/DEM. Top-level `dem` lacks id/body/center/extent/native resolution, making the contradiction harder to resolve without the nested dossier. Repair: resolve the exported primary Earth site from the active mission/grid; expose catalogue selection separately only if useful, and attach explicit DEM identity/georeference/scale. This same rule must apply to Earth JSON, Markdown, spreadsheet and copy/provider summaries. Add an identity assertion rather than another screenshot-only check.

5. **Medium: standalone AI dossier is scenario synthesis, not a report of the run.** `dossier.js:108` reloads terrain/weather and recalculates M2/M3. `dossier-context.mjs:35` has no input for session ID, preparation, landing, rover execution, inspection/evidence or future Earth expedition. Navigation in `index.html:23` carries no active scenario/session. `gridSummary:4` also omits gridSize/spacing/extent, important when Discovery candidates can have 2400 m versus 7200 m windows; `auditSummary:23` drops row evidence. Consequently a generated M5 document cannot substantiate claims about the operator's actual choices/run or reconcile to the local JSON/MD/XLSX cutoff. Repair additively: accept a compact immutable active-run snapshot with a cutoff and source session; retain standalone scenario synthesis with its scope explicitly identified. Preserve scale and evidence references in the provider summary. No existing copy-context control was found in baseline first-party JS/HTML; add it to the new expedition if required, using the same snapshot rather than an independently assembled text report.

6. **Integration trap: Earth or Discovery IDs cannot be substituted into the destination M2 pipeline.** `analog-audit.mjs:79 validateCandidateAnalogPayload` deliberately requires scenario.body moon/mars; `mission-profiles.mjs:192 getMissionBaseProfile` throws outside four fixed scenario IDs; `analog-audit-ui.mjs recompute` calls `missionDesignEngineering(candidate)`; `xlsx-report.mjs:92 missionSheets` requires a matching fixed base profile and a destination session. The remote dossier's synthetic Discovery IDs work because it uses only `automaticEngineering`, not those profile/XLSX paths. Repair the new Earth expedition with an additive independent run/report contract and an explicitly referenced destination benchmark; reuse pure calculation/inspection utilities, not fabricated destination identities or a fake Earth landing. Preserve automatic M2 observations, proposed design, manual lab and mesh presentation as separate sources.

7. **Existing data-honesty hazards to avoid carrying into the new decision surface.** `mission.js:2250 renderPhysicsPlain` says a low interpolated tilt means “Sin riesgo de vuelco” and turns unknown thermal readings into a paragraph saying “Dentro del rango”; missing pitch/roll become zero tilt. Neither follows from regional DEM and absent thermal data. `updatePhysics:1815`/`physicsBattery:1866` are split-only, so merely extending the existing Terrain UI leaves its battery on the older fixed kinematic drain while a weather scene may imply a physical energy result. `updateWeather`/`weatherRover` can use an explicit simulated preset while `updatePhysics` labels solar using the provider telemetry label. Repair touched Earth surfaces to show regional indicators/unknowns and the actual active modeled source; either implement and label the Earth energy model or call the existing battery behavior illustrative. Missing sunlight must not silently become an observed zero. These are substantive source/decision semantics, not translation/style requests.
